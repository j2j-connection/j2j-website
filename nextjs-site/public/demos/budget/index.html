WHAT THIS WORKFLOW SHOWS

AI-assisted project-budget analysis, with the figures in reach.

A budget can be under its total allowance while one consultant is already over its approved fees. This walkthrough shows how a conversational interface could help a project team find that exception and prepare the next question.

Start with the approved baseline

The sample has $275,000 in original consultant fees and $25,000 in approved changes. Against the resulting $300,000 budget, $234,000 has been billed. The $66,000 balance is unbilled budget, not a forecast of final cost or proof that the project is on track.

Check each workstream, not just the total

Architectural design has $90,000 in approved fees and $99,000 billed, a $9,000 difference. Other workstreams still have budget remaining, which can hide that exception in the overall balance. The source link lets you inspect the row behind the answer.

Use the answer to ask a better question

The draft email asks the team to reconcile the difference. It does not assume whether the cause is extra scope, an invoice error, or an approval missing from the tracker. Someone needs to check the supporting records before acting.

What would change with your own files?

We would first agree which records are authoritative, how approved changes are recorded, who can access the data, and where human review belongs. A live system needs tested calculations and source references alongside any AI-generated explanation.

This is a demonstration, not a live AI service. It uses one fictional workbook and three prepared responses. It does not accept uploads, review your project, or send emails.
